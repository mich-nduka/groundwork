# 6. Operations & Scalability

## Operating model
Who runs the system day to day, and what are their routine jobs?

## Environments and deployment
The environments (dev, staging, production...), what gets released as a unit, and how configuration is handled.

## Observability
- Logs: structured fields, with sensitive data left out.
- Metrics: health, usage, latency, errors and business numbers.
- Traces: where following a request across components is worth it.
- Alerts: conditions worth acting on, how severe, and who owns them.

## Diagnostics and health
Health checks, readiness probes, debugging information and runbooks (step-by-step guides for handling incidents).

## Scaling strategy
Where the bottlenecks will show up first, and the least complex way to scale that meets demand.

## Operational risks
Costs, vendor lock-in, maintenance burden, recovery time and capacity risks.
